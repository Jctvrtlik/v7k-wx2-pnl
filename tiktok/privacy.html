Privacy Policy

SKYE Investment Group / JB Partners, LLC · Last updated: July 8, 2026

This policy explains how the "SKYE Social Reporting" application ("the App") handles data. The App is an internal tool used by SKYE Investment Group and its affiliate JB Partners, LLC ("we," "us") to produce weekly performance reports for TikTok accounts that we own or manage for our multifamily properties.

What the App accesses

With the account holder's authorization through TikTok's official Login Kit and Display API, the App reads: basic public profile information (such as display name and avatar) and a list of the account's videos together with their public statistics (views, likes, comments, and shares). The App requests the permissions user.info.basic and video.list only.

What the App does NOT do

The App is read-only. It cannot post, edit, delete, message, or change anything on any account. It does not access private messages, and it does not collect passwords or login credentials.

How we use the data

The information is used solely for our internal weekly reporting. We do not sell it, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising or targeting.

Storage and access

Data is stored securely and is accessible only to authorized SKYE personnel. Access tokens are kept confidential and are used only to retrieve the statistics described above.

Revoking access

An account holder can remove the App's access at any time from within TikTok (Settings → Security & permissions → Apps), which immediately stops all data access.
